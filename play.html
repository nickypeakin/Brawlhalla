How to Play

Knock opponents off the stage. Every hit raises their damage %, and the higher it is the further they fly. Last fighter with stocks left wins.

  • Lights change with direction: neutral, side and down, on the ground and in the air.
  • Heavies on the ground are big Signature moves. In the air, heavy = Recovery (once per jump), down + heavy = Ground Pound.
  • Grab the glowing weapons that fall from the sky (light attack or throw over them). Each legend has two weapons with their own moves. Throw them at people!
  • You get 3 jumps. Hold toward a wall to cling to it and wall-jump. Press down in the air to fast fall, or on a platform to drop through.
  • Getting hit refreshes your jumps, dodge and recovery, so you can always fight back.
  • In local play, two people can share a keyboard (WASD side and Arrows side) and up to 4 gamepads are supported.
